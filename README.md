## Prop firm software

White-label platforms and back offices for running challenges, funded accounts, checkout and payouts. Some vendors sell software and leave trader payouts to you; others run the firm with you and cover payouts in return for a share of sales. Read each vendor's terms before you sign.

- [Axcera](https://axcera.io/) - Prop firm software that combines a CRM, trading infrastructure and risk management, and connects to third-party CFD and futures platforms.
- [Brokeree Prop Pulse](https://brokeree.com/solutions/prop-pulse/) - Account management solution for prop firms on MetaTrader and cTrader, for setting up multi-step trader evaluation challenges.
- [FPFX Tech](https://fpfxtech.com/) - Prop firm technology that automates challenge and account workflows, with integrations for platforms including MT5, cTrader, DXtrade, Match-Trader, TradeLocker and Rithmic.
- [FXPropTech](https://fxproptech.com/) - White-label prop firm platform with an evaluation engine, risk management, trader dashboard, CRM and automated payouts.
- [Match-Prop](https://match-prop.com/) - Program for launching a branded prop firm on Match-Trade Technologies' technology, with the operator handling payments, KYC, trader support and payouts.
- [PropAccount](https://propaccount.com/) - White-label prop firm provider offering trading platforms and risk tools, with funded accounts backed by its own capital on its plans.
- [PropExecutor](https://www.propexecutor.com/) - Prop firm software built around its own browser trading terminal, with a prop dashboard and rule checks in every trading account.
- [PropSim](https://propsim.markets/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=awesome-prop-firms) - White-label prop firm software with a challenge and risk engine, branded trader terminal, CRM, KYC and payouts in one platform.
- [PropSuite](https://propsuite.com/) - White-label prop firm program that provides the platform and says it funds traders and covers payouts.
- [Tickblaze](https://www.tickblaze.com/) - Multi-asset trading platform with an order management system, trade copier and prop firm CRM, for launching or migrating a prop firm.
- [Trade Tech Solutions](https://www.tradetechsolutions.io/) - Prop firm technology covering platform connections, CRM, payments, risk tools and websites, for CFD, futures, crypto and other kinds of prop firm.
- [YourPropFirm](https://yourpropfirm.com/) - Prop firm operating platform with a CRM, risk and rule engine, challenge flows, payouts, KYC and a branded trader portal, plus optional paid services.

## Trading platforms

The platforms traders use to trade their evaluation and funded accounts.

- [cTrader](https://www.spotware.com/) - Spotware's FX and CFD trading platform and technology stack for brokers and prop firms.
- [DXtrade](https://dx.trade/) - Devexperts' multi-asset trading platform for brokers, with prop trading technology for CFDs and futures.
- [Match-Trader](https://match-trader.com/) - Match-Trade Technologies' trading platform for brokers, with a prop trading edition and a prop CRM for challenges and evaluations.
- [MetaTrader 5](https://www.metatrader5.com/) - MetaQuotes' multi-asset platform for forex, stocks and futures, licensed to brokers as a server and used by traders on desktop, web and mobile.
- [NinjaTrader](https://ninjatrader.com/) - Futures brokerage and trading platform with desktop, web and mobile apps.
- [Quantower](https://www.quantower.com/) - Multi-asset, broker-neutral trading platform for CFDs, futures, options, stocks and crypto.
- [TradeLocker](https://tradelocker.com/) - Web and mobile trading platform for forex and CFDs, offered to brokers and prop firms.
- [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/) - Charting platform and social network for traders, with trading through connected brokers.
- [Tradovate](https://www.tradovate.com/) - Futures brokerage and trading platform, part of NinjaTrader.
- [Volumetrica Trading](https://www.volumetricatrading.com/) - Volume-analysis trading platforms for futures and other markets, plus dashboards for prop firm operations and monitoring.

### Futures data and connectivity

- [CQG](https://www.cqg.com/) - Trading platforms, market data and order-routing technology for futures and options markets.
- [Rithmic](https://www.rithmic.com/) - Trading infrastructure, market data and connectivity for futures and options.

## MetaTrader APIs and bridges

### APIs

- [fxapis](https://fxapis.com/) - Hosted MetaTrader 5 REST API for trading and reading MT5 accounts, including evaluation and funded accounts, with no VPS or terminal to run.
- [MetaApi](https://metaapi.cloud/) - Cloud REST and WebSocket API for MetaTrader 4 and 5 accounts, with a copy-trading API.
- [MetaTrader 5 Python integration](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/python_metatrader5) - Official reference for the MetaTrader5 Python package, which exchanges data and trade requests with a running MT5 terminal.
- [MQL5 Reference](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs) - Official documentation for MQL5, the language for MetaTrader 5 indicators, expert advisors and scripts.

### Liquidity bridges

- [Brokeree Solutions](https://brokeree.com/) - MetaTrader 4/5 and cTrader plugins and platform servicing, including liquidity bridges for MetaTrader and DXtrade.
- [Centroid Solutions](https://www.centroidsol.com/) - Bridge, risk management, hosting and CRM modules for multi-asset brokers, with a prop trading offering.
- [Gold-i](https://www.gold-i.com/) - MatrixNet liquidity bridge, MetaTrader plugins, risk management and MT4/MT5 hosting for brokers, with a prop technology offering.
- [oneZero](https://www.onezero.com/) - Multi-asset execution, connectivity and distribution technology for retail brokers, institutions and liquidity providers.
- [PrimeXM](https://www.primexm.com/) - XCore bridge that connects MT4, MT5 and other platforms to liquidity providers, with hosting in financial hubs.
- [Tools for Brokers](https://t4b.com/) - Trade Processor liquidity bridge for MT4, MT5, cTrader, Match-Trader and DXtrade, plus risk and reporting tools for brokers and prop firms.

## Market data and liquidity

- [Databento](https://databento.com/) - Real-time and historical market data APIs for futures, equities, options and more.
- [dxFeed](https://dxfeed.com/) - Real-time, historical and calculated market data via APIs for stocks, derivatives, forex, crypto and other assets.
- [Massive](https://massive.com/) - Market data APIs for stocks, currencies, futures and more, formerly Polygon.io.
- [TraderMade](https://tradermade.com/) - Real-time and historical forex, crypto and CFD prices via REST, WebSocket and FIX.
- [Twelve Data](https://twelvedata.com/) - Real-time and historical stock, forex, crypto and fundamental data APIs.

### Liquidity providers

For firms that route some funded traders' orders to live markets.

- [B2Broker](https://b2broker.com/) - Prime-of-prime liquidity and technology provider for forex, CFD and crypto brokers.
- [Finalto](https://www.finalto.com/) - Liquidity, prime brokerage and trading technology for brokers and institutions.
- [LMAX Group](https://www.lmax.com/) - Operator of institutional exchanges and liquidity for FX and crypto.

## Payments

Check each provider's terms for prop firms and trading-related businesses before you integrate.

### Cards and payment orchestration

- [Nuvei](https://www.nuvei.com/) - Payments platform for pay-ins and payouts across cards and local payment methods.
- [Paytiko](https://paytiko.com/) - Payment orchestration platform that routes pay-ins, payouts and refunds across connected payment providers.
- [Praxis Tech](https://praxis.tech/) - Payment orchestration platform that connects merchants to many payment service providers through one integration.

### Crypto payments

- [B2BinPay](https://b2binpay.com/) - Crypto payment gateway for accepting crypto, with conversion to fiat payouts.
- [BitPay](https://bitpay.com/) - Crypto payment processor for merchants, plus a wallet for buying, storing and spending crypto.
- [BTCPay Server](https://btcpayserver.org/) - Self-hosted, open-source cryptocurrency payment processor.
- [Coinbase Business](https://www.coinbase.com/business) - Coinbase's platform for businesses to accept and send crypto payments, formerly Coinbase Commerce.
- [CoinPayments](https://www.coinpayments.net/) - Crypto payment infrastructure for merchants and businesses.
- [Confirmo](https://confirmo.com/) - Crypto and stablecoin payment gateway for businesses.
- [Match2Pay](https://match2pay.com/) - Multi-crypto payment gateway that lets clients deposit and withdraw in crypto.
- [NOWPayments](https://nowpayments.io/) - Crypto payment gateway for accepting Bitcoin, USDT, Ethereum and other coins.
- [Triple-A](https://www.triple-a.io/) - Stablecoin and local-currency payments and payouts infrastructure for businesses.

### Trader payouts

- [Rise](https://www.riseworks.io/) - Global contractor-payment platform that pays people in local currencies or crypto, used by some prop firms for trader payouts.

## KYC and identity verification

- [ComplyAdvantage](https://complyadvantage.com/) - AML and fraud risk detection for screening customers and monitoring transactions.
- [Didit](https://didit.me/) - Identity and fraud API with modules for KYC, AML screening and wallet screening.
- [Jumio](https://www.jumio.com/) - Identity verification platform for customer onboarding, fraud prevention and compliance.
- [Persona](https://withpersona.com/) - Platform for verifying people and businesses (KYC, KYB, AML) with configurable verification flows.
- [Shufti](https://shuftipro.com/) - Identity verification and AML screening for KYC compliance across many countries and languages.
- [Sumsub](https://sumsub.com/) - Verification platform for users, businesses and transactions, with case management and fraud prevention.
- [Veriff](https://www.veriff.com/) - Identity verification that checks documents and biometrics and screens databases.

## Risk and fraud prevention

Tools for catching multi-accounting, VPN and proxy use, payment fraud and chargebacks.

- [Fingerprint](https://fingerprint.com/) - Device intelligence platform that identifies browsers and mobile devices, including anonymous visitors.
- [IPQualityScore](https://www.ipqualityscore.com/) - Proxy, VPN, bot and email-risk detection APIs for fraud prevention.
- [MaxMind](https://www.maxmind.com/) - IP geolocation and proxy data, plus fraud and chargeback screening.
- [SEON](https://seon.io/) - Real-time fraud detection and AML screening in one platform.
- [Sift](https://sift.com/) - Fraud prevention platform for payment fraud and account takeover.

## Legal, regulation and industry reading

None of this is legal advice. The rules for prop firms differ by country, so get advice for the places you sell in.

### Regulators and court cases

- [CFTC: complaint against My Forex Funds (2023)](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8771-23) - The US regulator's press release announcing its fraud complaint against Traders Global Group, the operator of My Forex Funds.
- [Consob: notice on "funded trading" (2024)](https://www.consob.it/web/area-pubblica/-/comunicazione-del-2024-07-08) - The Italian regulator's notice to savers about "funded trading" offers from firms that call themselves prop firms (in Italian).
- [FSMA: warning on prop trading firms](https://www.fsma.be/en/warnings/fsma-warns-consumers-against-shadow-investment-game) - The Belgian regulator's warning about the risks of prop trading firms' paid challenges, which it calls a "shadow investment game".
- [Willkie: court dismisses the CFTC's My Forex Funds case (2025)](https://complianceconcourse.willkie.com/articles/new-jersey-federal-judge-dismisses-forex-case-with-prejudice-and-awards-attorney-fees/) - A law firm's summary of the May 2025 order that dismissed the CFTC's case against Traders Global Group with prejudice and awarded fees on the sanctions motion.

### Industry news and analysis

- [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/) - Trading industry news site with regular coverage of prop firms, brokers and trading platforms.
- [Finance Magnates: Czech regulator on prop firms and MiFID (2024)](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/exclusive-czech-regulator-asserts-prop-trading-firms-may-be-subject-to-mifid/) - Report of the Czech National Bank's statement that some prop trading business models may fall under MiFID.
- [Finance Magnates: prop firms change platforms (2024)](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/prop-trading-firms-renaissance-cutting-us-clients-integrating-new-trading-platforms/) - Report on prop firms dropping US clients and moving to cTrader, Match-Trader, DXtrade and TradeLocker after brokers ended their MetaTrader services.
- [FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/) - Industry news on FX and CFD brokers, trading platforms, regulation and prop firms.
- [Proprietary trading on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proprietary_trading) - Encyclopedia article on proprietary trading by banks and trading firms.

## Open-source tools

- [Lean](https://github.com/QuantConnect/Lean) - QuantConnect's algorithmic trading engine in Python and C#, for backtesting and live trading.
- [Lightweight Charts](https://github.com/tradingview/lightweight-charts) - TradingView's HTML5 canvas library for interactive financial charts.
- [NautilusTrader](https://github.com/nautechsystems/nautilus_trader) - Event-driven trading engine with a Rust core, for backtesting and live trading.
- [OpenApiPy](https://github.com/spotware/OpenApiPy) - Spotware's Python package for the cTrader Open API.
- [OpenCharts](https://github.com/dylanpersonguy/OpenCharts) - Self-hosted trading terminal that runs in the browser, built on Lightweight Charts, with drawing tools, indicators, a depth-of-market ladder and a paper-trading engine.
- [Prop Firm Calculators](https://github.com/PropSim-Enterprise/prop-firm-calculators) - Drawdown, position size, cost-to-get-funded, payout and prop firm revenue calculators from PropSim, with tested formulas and an embeddable mode.
- [Prop Firm Landing Page Templates](https://github.com/PropSim-Enterprise/prop-firm-landing-page-templates) - Ten React and Tailwind CSS landing page templates for prop firms from PropSim, with challenge pricing, rules tables, an FAQ and matching sign-up and sign-in pages built from one config file.
- [Prop Firm Pass Rates](https://github.com/PropSim-Enterprise/prop-firm-pass-rates) - Weekly open dataset (CSV and JSON, CC BY 4.0) from PropSim of challenge pass rates, failure causes and account survival on firms using its platform.
- [Prop Firm Risk Panel](https://github.com/PropSim-Enterprise/prop-firm-tradingview-indicator) - TradingView indicator in Pine Script from PropSim that sizes a position to the risk and shows the losing trades left to the daily loss limit and maximum loss.
- [Prop Firm Sim](https://github.com/LuxAlgo/prop-firm-sim) - LuxAlgo's Monte Carlo simulator that estimates the odds of passing a prop firm challenge under a firm's exact rules.
- [prop-firm-rules](https://github.com/PropSim-Enterprise/prop-firm-rules) - TypeScript library from PropSim that evaluates challenge rules (profit target, daily loss, maximum and trailing drawdown, trading days, consistency) against an account's balance and equity history.
- [PropForge](https://github.com/michaelsboost/PropForge) - Offline, browser-based prop firm challenge simulator for MNQ and NQ futures with phase-based rules.
- [TradeNote](https://github.com/Eleven-Trading/TradeNote) - Self-hostable trading journal for storing and reviewing trades.

## For prop traders

### Comparison and review sites

Many comparison sites carry discount codes or partner offers from the firms they list. Read a firm's own rules and terms before you pay.

- [Forex Peace Army](https://www.forexpeacearmy.com/) - Long-running forum and review site where traders post reviews of brokers and prop firms.
- [Prop Firm Compare](https://propfirm.compare/) - Comparison of futures prop firms by drawdown rules, profit splits, payouts and funding size.
- [Prop Firm Match](https://propfirmmatch.com/) - Prop firm comparison site with filters for platforms, assets and payout methods, plus trader reviews.
- [PropFirmMap](https://propfirmmap.com/) - Side-by-side comparison of prop trading firms with reviews, ratings and pricing.

### Prop firm coupon codes

Discount codes change often. Check a code's terms on the firm's own checkout before you pay.

- [Prop firm coupon codes](https://propsim.markets/prop-firm-coupon-codes?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=awesome-prop-firms) - Directory from PropSim of current discount codes for more than 100 prop firms, with each code labelled by how sure the check is that it works and the date the codes were last checked.

### Rules, risk and calendars

- [Babypips Position Size Calculator](https://www.babypips.com/tools/position-size-calculator) - Calculator for position size from account balance, risk percentage and stop-loss distance.
- [Forex Factory Calendar](https://www.forexfactory.com/calendar) - Forex-focused economic calendar of scheduled market-moving events, for planning around news-trading rules.
- [Maximum drawdown on Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/m/maximum-drawdown-mdd.asp) - Explainer of maximum drawdown and how it is calculated.

### Journals and analytics

- [Deltalytix](https://deltalytix.app/) - Trading journal for futures traders with broker sync and statistics, with its source on GitHub under CC BY-NC 4.0.
- [Edgewonk](https://edgewonk.com/) - Trading journal for forex, stocks, futures, crypto and CFDs, with trade import and analytics.
- [FX Blue](https://www.fxblue.com/) - Trading-results analysis, trade copiers, simulators and charts for forex traders.
- [Myfxbook](https://www.myfxbook.com/) - Trading account analysis with track-record verification for forex accounts.
- [TraderSync](https://tradersync.com/) - Trading journal for stocks, options, futures, forex and crypto, with broker sync and trade replay.
- [Tradervue](https://www.tradervue.com/) - Trading journal for stocks, options, futures and forex that finds patterns in your trading.
- [TradesViz](https://www.tradesviz.com/) - Trading journal and analytics platform with trade statistics and trade simulation.
- [TradeZella](https://www.tradezella.com/) - Trading journal with broker imports, setup tagging, analytics reports and session review.
- [TradeHarbor](https://tradeharbor.co/) - Trading journal for futures prop traders that imports trades from futures platforms and tracks each firm's drawdown, daily loss limit and consistency rules.
